Get started

It takes about a minute.

Get Jace Launcher

Download it and add your Microsoft Minecraft account.

Sign in to Jace Social

Open 👥 Friends and click Sign in. Mojang confirms it's really your account.

Add your friends

Click + Add friend and type their Minecraft username.

Play together

Chat, see who's online, and add Jace Friends to your instances to do it all in game.

Private by design

  • Sign-in works like joining a Minecraft server: Mojang confirms you own the account. Your password and game token are never sent to Jace Social.
  • Only real Microsoft accounts can sign in, so nobody can pretend to be you.
  • Only you and the friend you're talking to can read your messages.
  • Only friends see your status. Remove someone and they stop seeing it.
  • Live updates only say "something changed". Message text is fetched over your signed-in connection.

Questions

Can I use Jace Social with an offline account?

No. Friends and chat need a Microsoft (premium) account, because that's how we know the account is really yours.

Do my friends need Jace Launcher?

They need Jace Launcher or the Jace Friends mod to accept requests and chat. You can send them a request before they join, and they'll see it when they sign in.

Which Minecraft versions does the mod support?

Every release from 1.20.1 to 26.3, on Fabric, Quilt, NeoForge (1.20.4 and up) and Forge (1.20.1).

How does hosting a world work?

In a singleplayer world, open the Friends screen and click Host this world for friends. With the free e4mc mod installed, your world gets a public address, and your friends see a Join button. You don't need to change any router settings.

Is there a web version of chat?

Not yet. Signing in needs your Minecraft account, which only the launcher and the mod can confirm.

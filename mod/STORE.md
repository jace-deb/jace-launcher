# Jace Friends

Your [Jace Launcher](https://jace-store-deb.vercel.app/project/jace-launcher) friends list and chat, inside Minecraft.

## Features
- **Friends list in game:** press **J**, or click **👥 Friends** on the title or pause screen. See who's online and what they're doing: playing a version or server, or hosting a world.
- **Chat** with your friends without leaving the game. New messages and friend requests pop up as notifications.
- **Add friends** by Minecraft username, and accept or decline requests.
- **Host this world for friends:** opens your singleplayer world. With **[e4mc](https://modrinth.com/mod/e4mc)** installed it gets a public address automatically, and your friends see "Hosting …" with a **Join** button. No port forwarding needed.
- **Join** a friend's hosted world or server in one click.

The friends list is the same one as in Jace Launcher and syncs through Jace Social, so it follows your Minecraft account everywhere.

## Requirements
- Minecraft **26.3**, **Fabric Loader** 0.19+ and **Fabric API**
- A **Microsoft** (premium) account. Sign-in is verified by Mojang, the same way servers check you.
- Optional: **e4mc**, to host your world for friends outside your network

## Privacy
Your Minecraft login token is only ever sent to Mojang, never to Jace Social. Messages can only be read by you and the friend you're chatting with.
